# Landscape assessment and team handoff
Evidence date: 2026-11-10 America/New_York | Decision horizon: six months through 2027-05-10.

## Decision
Prepare a conditional offline/shadow comparison of an agent-to-solver advisory interface against incumbent APS. Do not replace scheduling infrastructure or authorize production changes from landscape evidence. All organization details are hypothetical. The most useful future signal is a comparable, traceable disruption-recovery deployment with disclosed constraints, repeated-run behavior, human authority and recovery outcomes; announcements alone warrant verification.

## Comparison
| Approach | Landscape position for this decision | What could change the choice |
|---|---|---|
| Dispatch rules | Transparent baseline/fallback; local rules may miss global tradeoffs | Demonstrated matched-case improvements without hidden feasibility loss |
| Existing APS and optimization | Strongest production comparator; S3 supplies a specific reported industrial case | Better local decision support at justified total cost under identical objectives |
| Learned dispatch and RL | Competing adaptive approach; local robustness is not established by this register | Relevant validated evaluation under product-mix and disruption changes |
| Agentic AI with solver and human approval | Candidate complementary orchestration layer; local net benefit unproven | Traceable replay evidence, validated state/constraints and recoverable integration |
| MES, replay/simulation, access controls | Shared complements; unreliable state undermines every approach | Engineering verification of data, constraints and fallback |

These relative positions are analytical inferences, not numerical maturity ratings. No current vendor league table or price comparison is established. S3 is vendor/customer reported; its specific objective and cycle-time tradeoff limit transfer. Exact source scope and URLs are in evidence/source_register.md.

## Priority selection
1. Comparable operational recovery evidence changes which pilot tests are worth funding.
2. MES/APS mapping and fallback determine whether the local comparison is credible.
3. Integration, inference, review and exit costs determine whether any incremental value survives.
Standards, vendor dependency, model version changes and jurisdiction are conditional gates. Popularity and fundraising alone do not establish value.

## Monitoring plan
All frequencies and role assignments are proposals requiring organizational ownership. These are instructions for future review, not scheduled automations.

- Proposed owner: technology-intelligence lead; monthly and on a named comparable deployment announcement; source: original customer technical report and auditable operational logs, using S3 as a scope-check example. Event: deployment evidence identifies model/tool versions, disruption conditions, constraint failures, recovery and human authority. Action: inspect comparability and revise pilot test coverage; publicity alone prompts verification, not expansion.

- Proposed owner: evaluation engineer; monthly and on candidate model releases; source: original model evaluations and research such as S2. Event: reproducible repeated-run or robustness improvement on relevant tasks. Action: replay matched local disruptions; benchmark progress alone does not establish operational readiness.

- Proposed owner: MES/APS integration lead; before every interface/model update and before advisory use; sources: installed interface specifications, local event/constraint logs and SEMI catalog S6. Event: state mapping changes, stale events, a hard constraint violation or failed fallback. Action: pause the affected path and revalidate; catalog revision alone triggers compatibility review, not forced migration.

- Proposed owner: procurement with operations; at each quote/renewal and monthly during the pilot; sources: original supplier quotes, dependency/exit terms and local review-time records, not currently supplied. Event: higher all-in cost, loss of version control or switching access changes the approved business case. Action: re-estimate economics and compare the simpler APS/workflow option; no invented savings cutoff.

- Proposed owner: governance lead; before operational use, quarterly and on applicable policy changes; sources: official regulator material once jurisdiction is known and NIST S5. Event: a newly applicable requirement or material access-boundary change. Action: obtain compliance review and modify or postpone the pilot as needed; NIST is voluntary guidance.
